About

I'm Caio Everest, a computer scientist and software engineer based in Rio de Janeiro. At Flutter Brazil, I lead engineering teams and continue developing software. My work combines backend engineering, distributed systems, and developer tools.

Across Flutter, will bank, CloudWalk, and Pismo, I've built systems for payments, banking, identity, and onboarding. I take work from design and implementation through deployment, on-call support, and production maintenance.

I actively develop internal AI developer tools and help engineers adopt them through talks and practical workshops. Outside work, I build personal tools for AI coding agents, OMP/Pi extensions, reusable skills, and integrations with the OpenRouter API.

I also co-organize GopheRio, the Go developer community in Rio de Janeiro, and enjoy building tools that remove repetitive work from software development.
